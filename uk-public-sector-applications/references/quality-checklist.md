# Quality checklist before handing over

Integrity
- [ ] Every claim traces to the CV, the user's words or their answers. No invented figures, team sizes, technologies or outcomes.
- [ ] Matrix vs direct leadership labelled accurately.
- [ ] Dates and figures the user should double-check are listed for them.

Coverage
- [ ] Every essential criterion has a clearly labelled section or opening sentence using its wording.
- [ ] Desirable criteria touched where evidence exists, without stealing words from essentials.
- [ ] Lead criterion / lead behaviour (if any) has the strongest example.

Pitch
- [ ] Scale (people, budget, users, availability, regulation) is stated for the main examples.
- [ ] Writing reads at the target grade's behaviour level (see success-profiles.md).
- [ ] Gaps are bridged honestly, not hidden.

Form
- [ ] Within the word or page limit; counts reported per section and in total.
- [ ] "I" throughout for personal actions.
- [ ] British English; acronyms expanded on first use; no untranslated sales jargon.
- [ ] No name or identifying details in statements if the process is name-blind (unless the form asks for them).
- [ ] CV explains gaps in the last two years if the pack asks.

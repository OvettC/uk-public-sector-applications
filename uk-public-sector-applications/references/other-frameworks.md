# Other UK public sector frameworks

Verify against the advert every time; frameworks are revised.

## NHS (Agenda for Change roles, via NHS Jobs / Trac)
- Sift is on the **person specification** (essential/desirable, often marked as assessed by application, interview or test) plus the **supporting information** box.
- Address every essential criterion in the supporting information, in the order listed, ideally with short headings.
- Weave in the NHS values (from the NHS Constitution) or the trust's own values, which most adverts list.
- Bands map roughly: Band 7 ≈ HEO/SEO–G7 scope, Band 8a–8d ≈ G7–G6, Band 9 / VSM ≈ SCS. Use the advert's scope, not the band, to pitch.

## Police (forces in England and Wales)
- Most forces use the College of Policing **Competency and Values Framework (CVF)**: values of impartiality, integrity, public service and transparency, and competencies grouped into resolute/compassionate/committed, inclusive/enabling/visionary, and intelligent/creative/informed clusters (e.g. "We are emotionally aware", "We take ownership", "We are collaborative", "We deliver, support and inspire", "We analyse critically", "We are innovative and open-minded"), each with levels.
- Police staff roles (e.g. Head of Architecture) usually ask for a supporting statement against the role profile plus CVF examples. Check which CVF level the advert specifies.
- Vetting (NPPV) is required; keep history accurate.

## Local government
- Person specification with essential/desirable criteria, often coded A (application), I (interview), T (test). Only "A" criteria are sifted — address all of them explicitly.
- Supporting statement is usually free-form; structure it with headings that match the person specification.
- Many councils use their own values/behaviours framework; mirror it if listed.

## Devolved governments, ALBs and NDPBs
- Scottish Government and Welsh Government use their own versions of Success Profiles (grade names differ). ALBs and NDPBs vary: follow the advert, and default to the person-specification approach if no framework is named.

# Personal statement structure (criteria-based)

**Opening (60–120 words)**
Why this role, in this organisation, now. Connect one or two genuine career threads to the department's stated strategy or mission. No generic praise.

**One section per essential criterion**
Heading: the criterion in short form (or the advert's exact words if short).
- Opening sentence that mirrors the criterion and states scale.
- Main example: situation in one or two sentences, then personal actions (most of the words), then measurable result.
- Optional supporting line from a different organisation for breadth.
- Optional final sentence linking to what the role needs (only if it adds something).

**Close (30–60 words)**
What the candidate would bring in the first year, in concrete terms. No repetition of earlier claims.

Word budget: weight sections toward criteria that need bridging or carry the most risk.
